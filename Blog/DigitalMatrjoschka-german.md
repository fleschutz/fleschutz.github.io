Digital fun with Attachment.zip
========================

**Who does not know the colorfully painted and nested wooden dolls from Russia. The wooden dolls enjoy great popularity as both toys and souvenirs and were first made in 1890. The digital counterpart is available free of charge here.**

**[Attachment.zip](/downloads/Attachment.zip)** (2 KB).

If you download this .ZIP file and then unzip it, you will then find the following file:

* `UNZIP_ME.zip` and then:
* `ARE_YOU_SURE.zip` and then:
* `NOT_UNZIPPED_YET.zip` and then:
* `STILL_WORK_LEFT.zip` and then:
* `ANOTHER_TRY.zip` and then:
* `DON'T_GIVE_UP.zip` and finally:
* `README.txt` with the content "Sorry, I forgot to include the attachment ;-)".

The .ZIP file can of course be renamed as you like and then sent to your friends or colleagues. Have fun with it 😄
